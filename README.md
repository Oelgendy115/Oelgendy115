# 💫 About Me

🚀 **I'm currently working on:**  
I'm a 4th-year **Computer Systems Engineering student at Carleton University**, currently working with my teammates on two major engineering projects.

One is my **capstone project focused on hockey player analysis**, where we're using **UWB sensors, IMUs, and data processing techniques** to capture and analyze player movement and performance data.

I'm also working on an **autonomous snowplow robot**, designed to navigate and operate using a combination of sensors including **ultrasonic sensors, IR sensors, IMUs, Time-of-Flight sensors, and more**. I enjoy working across both the hardware and software sides of a system and seeing a design progress from an idea into a functioning physical system.

🤝 **I'm looking to collaborate on:**  
I'm interested in projects involving **embedded systems, FPGA development, digital hardware design, sensor integration, robotics, computer architecture, and low-level systems development**. I especially enjoy projects where software interacts directly with hardware and where designs can be implemented, tested, and improved in the real world.

💼 **Actively Seeking New Job Opportunities:**  
I'm open to opportunities in areas related to **Embedded Systems, Semiconductor Engineering, FPGA Development, Digital Design, Verification, Computer Hardware, and Systems Engineering**.

I'm particularly interested in roles involving **Verilog, HDL development, FPGA design, embedded software, hardware-software integration, computer architecture, and semiconductor technologies**. I enjoy designing systems, understanding how they work at a low level, and seeing those designs come to fruition.

🛠️ **I'm looking to grow in:**
